# 003 — Leave-one-day-out with all undamaged recordings

## Question and data

How well does the model transfer to a day it has never seen when it is trained on all the other undamaged data?

`002` trains on one day (22 September) only. This experiment uses every undamaged (`UDS`) recording: the eight sine sweeps (`SM`) and the two noise recordings (`NM`). The recordings come from three days. The damaged recordings are not used. The target is `SB01_x` in MPa, the inputs are the 75 bridge acceleration channels at 100 Hz. [HBTA dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/14632942)

| Day | Recordings | Duration (s) | Temperature | Wind | Train passage |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 22 Sep | `P1/Y_02`, `P1/Z_02`, `P2/Y_02`, `P2/Z_02`, `NM_Z_02` | 3,439 | 10 – 11 °C | Low | Yes |
| 23 Sep | `P1/Y_01`, `P1/Z_01` | 1,246 | 15 °C | Medium | No |
| 27 Sep | `P2/Y_01`, `P2/Z_01`, `NM_Z_01` | 2,194 | 13 – 14 °C | Low | No |

Durations are the full recording lengths (sweeps and noise), from the HDF5 metadata.

## Split

Three folds. In each fold one day is the test set and the other two days are the training data.

| Test day | Training days | Training duration (s) |
|---|---|---:|
| 22 Sep | 23 Sep + 27 Sep | 3,440 |
| 23 Sep | 22 Sep + 27 Sep | 5,633 |
| 27 Sep | 22 Sep + 23 Sep | 4,685 |

Every shaker setting (`P1/Y`, `P1/Z`, `P2/Y`, `P2/Z`) appears in the training data of every fold. Each recording is scored once, by a model that has not seen its day. Temperature, wind and train passage change together with the day, so the scores measure transfer across these changes together and cannot separate them.

## Validation within a fold

The test day is excluded from everything: fitting, preprocessing statistics, model selection and early stopping. Inside the training days, the validation set for early stopping and model selection is made of whole recordings, never of windows from a recording that is also used to fit. The exact rotation of the validation recordings is fixed before any test day is scored.

## Model and preprocessing

The model of `002` (causal TCN with batch normalisation, windows of 5 s with 75% overlap), frozen after `002`. Standardisation of the acceleration channels and of the strain: mean and std are fitted on the training recordings of the fold only and applied unchanged to the validation and test recordings. The predictions are converted back to MPa before the scores.

## Evaluation

- RMSE in MPa and R² per test recording, and the mean over the recordings of the fold. Constant baseline: the mean of the training targets of the fold. Also the correlation and the R² after the best multiplicative gain, as in `002`.
- Report the sweeps and the noise recordings separately. The noise recordings have a different excitation, and a noise recording is scored only in the fold of its own day.
- The folds have different amounts of training data and different test durations. Report the scores per day. Do not average the days into one number without saying so.
- Freeze the evaluation and model selection procedure before scoring any test day.

## Limits

- The strain amplitude differs between days (see `002`), so a model can learn the amplitude of the training days and fail on the test day. The 22 September fold is the one where the test amplitude is the smallest.
- The noise recordings add data, but with a single shaker setting (`P2/Z`).
- Only three test days exist, so the spread of the scores across folds is a rough measure.
