# 002 — Input normalisation and batch normalisation

## Question and data

Does standardising the acceleration channels and adding batch normalisation to the TCN let the model beat a constant prediction on the `001` split?

Same recordings and split as `001`: the eight undamaged (`UDS`) sine sweeps (`SM`). Train on the four `_02` recordings (22 September 2020), test on the four `_01` recordings (23 and 27 September 2020), paired by shaker setting (`P1/Y`, `P1/Z`, `P2/Y`, `P2/Z`). The target is `SB01_x` in MPa. The inputs are the 75 bridge acceleration channels at 100 Hz. There is no PCA. [HBTA dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/14632942)

Reference implementation: `001.ipynb` (per-channel mean removed, no scaling, no batch normalisation). The implementation of this experiment is `002.ipynb`.

## What changes with respect to 001

1. **Input standardisation.** Each acceleration channel is standardised, `(x - mean) / std`. Mean and std come from the fit recordings only (the three fit recordings in a validation fold, the four training recordings in the final fit) and are applied unchanged to validation and test. A small constant (`1e-8`) is added to the std so that a dead channel does not divide by zero. The target is not scaled in the main run.
2. **Batch normalisation.** A `BatchNormalization` layer (momentum 0.9) follows the input projection and every dilated convolution, before the skip sum.

Everything else is as in `001`.

## Windows and model

- Windows of 5 s (500 samples), one every 125 samples (75% overlap). The target of a window is the strain at its last sample. Each window stays within one recording. Windows are cut after the recordings are assigned to fit, validation and test.
- Causal temporal convolutional network: pointwise projection to 16 channels, eight residual blocks (causal `Conv1D`, 16 channels, kernel 5, dilations 1, 2, 4, ..., 128, batch normalisation, skip sum, ReLU), max pooling of size 2 after the second and fourth block, the last time step, and `Dense(1)`. About 12,000 trainable parameters.
- Adam (learning rate 0.001), mean squared error, batches of 32, at most 200 epochs, early stopping on the validation loss (patience 20) with the best weights restored. Seed 0.

## Validation within the training set

As in `001`: four folds, each holding out one whole `_02` recording (one shaker setting) for validation. The `_01` recordings stay out of every fold. The final fit uses all four `_02` recordings for the median number of best epochs found in the folds. It has no validation set.

## Evaluation

- RMSE in MPa and R² per test recording, and the equally weighted mean of the four recordings. Constant baseline: the mean of the training window targets.
- Diagnostics, because the first runs did not beat the baseline:
  - Pearson correlation, and R² after the best multiplicative gain on the predictions. The second one removes a wrong amplitude and keeps the shape.
  - The same scores on the training recordings, and the predictions over time for one training and one test recording.
  - A ridge regression without a network, on the same folds, for two tasks: the envelope (log10 of the strain RMS in each window, from log band powers of the 75 channels) and the instantaneous strain (from lagged acceleration samples). It tells whether the accelerations carry information about the strain.
- Quick check of a standardised target: the strain is standardised with the mean and std of the training windows, the network predicts the standardised value, and the predictions are converted back to MPa before the scores. It uses a fixed number of epochs and no folds.

## Data note: the strain amplitude differs between days

Every recording has a strain mean of 0 in the file, but the spread is not the same.

| Recordings | Date | Std of `SB01_x` (MPa) |
|---|---|---|
| `_02` sweeps | 22 Sep | 0.058 – 0.082 |
| `_01` sweeps, `P1` | 23 Sep | 0.148 – 0.153 |
| `_01` sweeps, `P2` | 27 Sep | 0.178 – 0.198 |
| `UDS_NM_Z_02` (noise) | 22 Sep | 0.057 |
| `UDS_NM_Z_01` (noise) | 27 Sep | 0.170 |

The `_01` strain is 2.5 to 3 times larger, while the acceleration RMS of a reference channel is similar. The relation between acceleration and strain therefore changes between days, for a reason that is not known. A model trained on 22 September cannot reproduce the amplitude of the other days, and the test scores of this split mix this effect with the quality of the model.

## Limits

- The test recordings were scored during development. The test scores are not used to choose among variants; the choices are made on the validation folds.
- Windows overlap by 75%, so the number of windows overstates the independent information.
- The final fit has no validation set, so its number of epochs is fixed from the folds.
