# 005 — Removing temporal max pooling

Does preserving all 500 time positions improve held-out endpoint strain estimates? [004](004.md) max-pooled after residual blocks 2 and 4, reducing the time axis from 500 to 250 to 125. In 005, the only configuration change is `pool_after: []`. Both models have 11,601 trainable parameters and read out the last feature vector. Removing pooling also changes the spacing of later dilated convolutions in input samples. Both networks' theoretical receptive fields cover the five-second input.

## Comparison

Use the [001 recording split](001.md) and the 004 training protocol. Fit on the four undamaged `_02` sine sweeps, using all 247,118 eligible endpoints in the final fit. For model selection, hold out each `_02` recording in turn. With batches of 512, check the validation MSE eight times per pass and select among checkpoints after at least one full pass. Refit for the rounded median best pass. Score the four `_01` test recordings at the same 1,981 timestamps as 004. Report RMSE in MPa and R² per recording and their equally weighted means.
